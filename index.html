Dear
Rithesh
If someone told me during that NCC camp that among all the sweat, drill shouts, and chaotic cultural prep, I’d find a friend like you, I wouldn’t have believed them. That camp gave me some of my worst stress, but standing beside you as MCs for a thousand cadets made every insane minute worth it.
You were different from anyone I’d met—bubbly, genuinely kind, and completely grounded. When I made mistakes or ranted about my life, you just sat there and listened without a single shred of judgment. And when fever hit me right in the middle of our event and I couldn’t even stand straight, you checked my forehead, stayed right there, and literally stuffed a tea cake in my mouth because we were running on zero fuel. In a place so rigid, you were pure warmth.
When camp ended and you went back to school, I spent those three months quietly hoping you wouldn't forget me. And when you reached out, my joy was beyond words. You helped me with my stuff effortlessly, without asking for anything in return. But I want you to know this isn't a one-way street—we are a team. We held down a massive stage together as equals, and that alliance doesn't end at school gates or board exams.
Once my hand recovers and these syllabus bounds fade, whether I come visit or we build something across screens, we are going to make things happen—writing a survey paper, coding a new app, or just exploring somewhere new. Distance is just temporary logistics.
Take care at residential school, Rithesh. Keep that spark alive. I’m proud to call you my friend and my partner in all of this.
